1.1 Chapter 1

FADE IN:

EXT. THE ANCIENT GROVE - CHAPTER 1

EPISODE 1: Episode 1. CHAPTER 1.1 — Chapter 1.

The camera takes the field. Wide.

The ancient grove shudders. A darkness creeps through the roots, twisting all it touches.

The camera finds them — closer now.

A beat.

STUMPEY
(scared)

Big problems... we oughtta get outta here.

LADY
(whispering)

What is happening to the forest? I can feel it dying.

DOC
(quietly, to himself)

Something has poisoned the heartwood. There is no curing this from here.

GOBLIN
(shouting)

Less talking, more running! North, through the old pass!

STUMPEY
(commanding)

Right. Stay close everyone. Move!

CUT TO:

PLAY
  • REACH THE OLD ROAD (NORTH) — The old road. Nobody's used it in ages.

CUT TO:

EXT. THE ANCIENT GROVE - CHAPTER 1 - LATER

The camera takes the field. Wide.

STUMPEY
(whispering)

We made it...

LADY
(scared)

But look behind us...

The camera takes the field. Wide.

DOC
(quietly, to himself)

All those towers... everything we built. Gone.

A beat.

GOBLIN

No use cryin' about it. What's ahead?

STUMPEY

The old road. Nobody's used it in ages.

The party turns north, leaving the corrupted grove behind.

A beat.

FADE OUT.

1.2 Chapter 2

FADE IN:

EXT. THE ANCIENT GROVE - CHAPTER 2

EPISODE 1: Episode 1. CHAPTER 1.2 — Chapter 2.

The camera takes the field. Wide.

A beat.

The old road opens onto another clearing. Watchtowers hold the shoulders. Something is already marching.

ANGLE ON STUMPEY.

STUMPEY
(commanding)

Towers left and right. Do not stop.

A beat.

CUT TO:

PLAY
  • TAKE THE NORTH RIDGE (EXIT) — Ridge is clear. Keep north.

CUT TO:

EXT. THE ANCIENT GROVE - CHAPTER 2 - LATER

The camera finds them — closer now.

DOC

Ridge is clear. Something still pulls north.

A beat.

FADE OUT.

FADE IN:

EXT. THE ANCIENT GROVE - CHAPTER 3

EPISODE 1: Episode 1. CHAPTER 1.3 — Chapter 3.

The camera takes the field. Wide.

A beat.

The ridge drops into a last hollow. The road ends in open dirt.

ANGLE ON STUMPEY.

GOBLIN
(shouting)

That bright patch. That is the door.

STUMPEY
(commanding)

Then we walk it. No more looking back.

A beat.

The camera finds them — closer now.

LADY

The blight cannot reach us here.

A beat.

The party stands on clean ground. This road is done.

A beat.

FADE OUT.
